**Status**: Accepted (2026-07-25)

**Implementation checkpoint (2026-07-29):** items 1–3 and 5 are implemented and held by the stored
GPT-5/GPT-5.6 QE suites: the induced end-to-end advocacy lifecycle, numeric 1–5 control with
first-run consultation, and visible empty-first precision all pass. Item 4 remains a publish-time
gate until npm, GitHub Release, and the plugin updater are live-verified at exactly 4.0.0; therefore
this ADR remains Accepted rather than being prematurely marked Implemented.

## The decision

**4.0.0 ships when the brain's proactivity is proven *correct* and put under *user control* — not when it
is proven *better* by a month of field outcomes.** ADR-042 defined the 4.0 gate as field-verified
advocacy precision (n≥29 real offer-outcomes) plus an independent re-grade. This ADR supersedes that
gate, on the owner's decision (2026-07-25), for a reason that is a *measurement*, not impatience:

> **The field-outcome gate is structurally unreachable on a single machine.** Measured 2026-07-25:
> `capability-audit` reports *"No dormant capability found on this machine."* The offer path
> (`anticipate.sh`) correctly stays silent when there is nothing dormant to advocate about — so on any
> healthy, actively-used machine the advocacy ledger (`advocacy-outcomes.jsonl`) never fills. The
> single row that ever existed was a test fixture (`id:"f-adv-1"`). "Wait for the soak" therefore has no
> bounded end on one machine: the healthier the setup, the fewer offers, the less data. n≥29 organic
> outcomes is a *fleet* metric, not a *pre-launch* one.

Recorded honestly: a bigger number for undone work is exactly what ADR-042 forbade, and this ADR does
**not** license that. It replaces an *unreachable* gate with a *provable* one — the version still may not
claim anything it hasn't earned (see "Honesty boundary").

## What must be true before the number becomes 4.0.0

1. **Proactivity is proven CORRECT, end-to-end.** The offer→record loop is demonstrated on an *induced*
   dormant case: a capability is put dormant, the brain offers it, the user acts, and the outcome lands
   in `advocacy-outcomes.jsonl` — shown, not argued. (This is the ADR-028 mechanism working; it is not a
   claim about field precision.)
2. **Proactivity is USER-CONTROLLED.** A 1–5 aggressiveness dial ("how much should I jump in?") with:
   a recommended default (Level 3, Balanced); honest, distinct per-level behaviour; a single enforcement
   chokepoint every offer channel reads; a console control; and a consultative first-run question. At
   levels 4–5 the brain may offer beyond just-dormant (optimizations, promotable lessons, tips) — which
   is *also* how the fleet ledger fills, from users who opted into more help.
3. **The self-measurement is visible and honest.** The console shows advocacy precision as *accruing*
   (empty-first, n and the honest interval), never a fabricated number, with the field-precision target
   labelled a post-launch metric.
4. **Release channels agree** (npm, GitHub release, plugin auto-update) — ADR-042 item 4, retained.
5. **The executive briefing exists** — ADR-042 item 5, retained.

Items 4–5 carry over unchanged from ADR-042. Items 1–3 replace ADR-042's items 1–3 (the field-outcome
gate).

## Honesty boundary — what 4.0 may and may NOT claim

Grounded in the GPT-5.6 cross-model duel (`docs/reviews/0042-gpt56-4.0-go-no-go.md`), which said 4.0 can
be honest *only if* ADR-042 is explicitly superseded (not ignored) and 4.0 redefined. This ADR is that
explicit supersession.

- **MAY claim:** honest, legible, fast, self-measuring; **proactivity you control**; a proven-correct
  offer-and-record mechanism; cross-project lessons that survive updates.
- **May NOT claim:** "demonstrably better than 3.x", "fully proactive/anticipatory (L3–L5) verified",
  "independently re-graded ≥95", "learns from real advocacy outcomes" (the ledger is still filling).

The `/whats-new` copy and the explainer are bound to the MAY list. `docs/4.0-READINESS.md` continues to
carry the field-precision gate as a **post-launch** row, so the honest number is never lost — it is
re-homed, not deleted.

## Why this is not the trap ADR-042 guarded against

ADR-042 existed to stop "a 3.5 with a bigger number." The test it applied — *is the claim backed?* —
still applies here, unchanged. The difference is only *which* claim 4.0 makes: ADR-042 assumed 4.0's
claim was "proven better by outcomes" (unbacked, and now known unreachable on one machine); this ADR
scopes 4.0's claim to "proactivity, proven correct and under your control" (backed by items 1–3). Same
honesty rule, a claim that can actually be earned by Monday.

## Consequences

- The 1–5 dial becomes the load-bearing new surface: it is simultaneously the user-control feature, the
  honesty mechanism (opt-in, so higher volume is consented, not nagging), and the fleet data engine.
- The field-precision metric (n≥29) moves to post-launch continuous improvement, surfaced in the console
  as accruing — the console's own empty-first honesty rule applies.
- ADR-042 is marked **Superseded by ADR-052**; its reasoning is preserved as the record of why the gate
  moved, not deleted.
